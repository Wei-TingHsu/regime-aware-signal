# Selling short under the three sell-off rules: how far the stock went against the position

Every past trade of SS-2a, SS-2b, SS-2c (2005-2026): the stock is sold short at the open after the sell-off and bought back at the rule's exit. 'Worst rise' = the highest price before the exit against the entry price. The account: US$70,000, all of it sold short in one stock (the worst case). The cushion = (account + money received - cost of buying the shares back) / cost of buying them back; a broker closes the position when the cushion falls under its limit. The founder's broker's limit for short sales is not known; 30% and 50% are shown. **A 2x inverse fund bought with half the money can lose at most US$35,000 and has no margin call; a 1x fund at most US$70,000.**
