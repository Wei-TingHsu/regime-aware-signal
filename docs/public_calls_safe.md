# SR-2c: public calls by well-known investors -- only what comes after the headline for certain

1011 calls priced; 952 of them (94%) carry a date but no real time of day. Day 0 = the first session on or after the headline's date. Returns in bp, minus the S&P 500's same piece, in the direction of the call. 'overnight into +1' = close of day 0 to the open of +1 (description: a headline that came out after the close shows up here, before a user can trade). 'open to close, +1' = buy at the open of the next session, sell at its close. 'open +1 to close +2' = hold one session longer. p = how often the same securities on 2000 sets of other dates did as well. A main cell counts at p < 0.0025 (0.05 / 10 groups / 2 pieces). Cost 30 bp a round trip (assumption).
