# N: netting -- one account per stock per afternoon

Signals after 16:00 from results releases, May 2018 to Nov 2026: the stock's own results score, and the directions other companies' releases give for it. **Each signal alone** = the first test's way (one unit per signal, 60 minutes each). **Net of signs** / **net of scores** = one account: after each signal the position is the sum of the signs (or of the scores) so far, limited to one unit, held until 60 minutes after the last signal. Returns in bp before cost; 'per unit' = total gain divided by the units of capital used.
