# Sell-off study, stage D: the machine search for patterns

Registered in TRACK 3.98 before running. Patterns are FOUND on sell-offs of 2005-2017 and CONFIRMED on 2018-2026 (not used for finding). A pattern = one, two or three conditions together, from one table or a pair of tables. FOUND: inside the pattern the outcome's frequency differs from its frequency over all sell-offs by z of 3.5 or more, on 60 or more day-equivalents (each day counts once however many securities fell). CONFIRMED: in 2018-2026 the difference has the same sign and at least half the size, on 30 or more day-equivalents. **The test:** the whole search is repeated 300 times on scrambled data (every sell-off day is given the outcomes of another day of similar size, a random number of places along the calendar). 'p' = the share of scrambled repeats that gave at least as many CONFIRMED patterns as the real data. A group of sell-offs counts at p < 0.0125 (0.05 / 4 groups). Only then are its patterns candidates, and only those seen in 24 or more separate months of 2005-2017 and 12 or more of 2018-2026 with a difference of 10 points or more in 2018-2026.
