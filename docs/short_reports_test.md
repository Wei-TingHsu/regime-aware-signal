# SR-1: short-report publishers -- how the company's share price moved

122 report-and-company rows priced (US-listed only; 39 rows on companies listed elsewhere left out of this run; 46 US symbols have no prices on Yahoo, mostly delisted). Returns in bp (1 bp = 0.01%), minus the S&P 500's same piece, IN THE DIRECTION OF THE REPORT: for a negative report a positive number means the share FELL by that much against the market. Day 0 = the session during which, or before whose open, the report came out (the publisher's own clock time). 'day 0' and '+1' to '+7' are each one session, close to close. 'gone before the open' = previous close to the open of day 0, for reports out before that open (description: not tradable in regular hours). 'open to close, day 0' = the same reports, from that open to the close. 'first tradable point to close +7' = from the open of day 0 (report out before the open) or from the close of day 0 (report out during the session) to the close of session +7. p = how often the same securities on 5000 sets of other dates did as well. Judged groups: 3; a cell counts at p < 0.00152. Cost 30 bp a round trip is an assumption and does NOT include the fee to borrow the shares for a short sale.
