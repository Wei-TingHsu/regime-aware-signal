# Exposure reading (EX-1): what the two reading steps would cost

Step 1, company profiles: 342 annual reports of 20 companies, 17.2 million characters -> between about US$12 and US$15.

Step 2, releases: 1316 TrendForce releases, 7.8 million characters -> between about US$9 and US$16.

Both: between about US$20 and US$31. Step 3 (the link to the income statement) is arithmetic and costs nothing.

Cheaper first step, if wanted: only the annual reports of the last 8 years and the releases since 2018 (the period the stocks of the list have prices and funds for): about US$11 to US$16.
