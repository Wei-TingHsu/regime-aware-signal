# The sell-off rules that passed, frozen (SS-2)

Each rule is one pattern of the machine search that PASSED test SO-T2 (the founder's real cost; `docs/selloff_real.md`). 'Frozen' = every cut and every machine group is written down as numbers in `docs/selloff_rules.json`, so the rule cannot drift when data is refreshed. The check column says whether the frozen rule picks exactly the sell-offs the search picked.

| code | when ALL of these hold on a day a stock of the list closes 5% or more down | trade | exit | sell-offs picked 2005-2017 / 2018-2026 | frozen rule = the search's pattern | passed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SS-2a | gap at the open %: low (under -2.94) AND in ai chain: yes AND machine group 3 of 5 in 'who fell and who set it off' (marked by low share of peers down 3pct and high peers average move pct) | SELL the stock at the next open | close 2 sessions after the sell-off | 91 / 94 | YES, exactly | against the S&P 500 and alone |
| SS-2b | machine group 3 of 5 in 'the fall and the market' (marked by high volume against normal and low size in units of its own usual move) AND news-based policy uncertainty index: low (under 111) AND St. Louis Fed financial stress index: low (under 0.397) | SELL the stock at the next open | close 2 sessions after the sell-off | 204 / 168 | YES, exactly | against the S&P 500 and alone |
| SS-2c | own results filing: yes AND size rank in the list: high (over 0.728) | SELL the stock at the next open | close 3 sessions after the sell-off | 166 / 214 | YES, exactly | against the S&P 500 only |
