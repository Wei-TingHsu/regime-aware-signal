# Sell-off study, test SO-T2: the strong patterns with the founder's real cost, US$70,000, and the single-stock funds

Registered in TRACK 3.104 before running. Account kept at US$70,000: each day's US$70,000 is split equally over that day's signals, whole shares at the real share price. Cost per order: the broker's fee board (platform US$0.99, settlement US$0.003 a share up to 1%, SEC fee on sells, audit-trail fee, 9% tax on the fees) **plus an allowance for the spread of 2 basis points per order for shares and 10 for funds (assumptions, not measured)**. Every SHORT position is charged 4.8% a year for the nights it is held -- the broker's rate for borrowed US dollars, used as a stand-in for the share-borrowing fee, which is not on the fee board. Buying a fund with the account's own money borrows nothing. All results are in basis points of the US$70,000 account per day with a trade (100 basis points = 1% = US$700). **PASSES** = the average is above zero in 2005-2017 and in 2018-2026, and in 2018-2026 the chance that it is zero or less is under 0.0021 (0.05 / 24 patterns; calendar months redrawn 5000 times). The patterns were chosen on these same years: a pass only admits a pattern to a forward record.
