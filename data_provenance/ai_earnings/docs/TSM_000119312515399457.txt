 TSMC November 2015 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Dec. 10, 2015 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for November 2015: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for November 2015 were approximately NT$63.43 billion, a decrease of 22.4 percent from October 2015 and a decrease of 12.2 percent versus November 2014. Revenues for January through November 2015 totaled NT$785.15 billion, an
increase of 13.2 percent compared to the same period in 2014.
