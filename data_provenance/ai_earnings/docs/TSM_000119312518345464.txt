 TSMC November 2018 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Dec. 10, 2018 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for November 2018: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for November 2018 were approximately NT$98.39 billion, a decrease of 3.1 percent from October 2018 and an increase of 5.6 percent from November 2017. Revenues for January through November 2018 totaled
NT$941.64 billion, an increase of 6.1 percent compared to the same period in 2017.
