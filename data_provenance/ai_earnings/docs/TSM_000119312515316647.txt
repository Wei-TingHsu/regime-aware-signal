 TSMC August 2015 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Sept. 10, 2015 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for August 2015: On a consolidated basis,
revenues for August 2015 were approximately NT$67.04 billion, a decrease of 17.2 percent from July 2015 and a decline of 3.2 percent versus August 2014. Revenues for January through August 2015 totaled NT$575.47 billion, an increase of 23.6 percent
compared to the same period in 2014.
