 TSMC December 2018 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Jan. 10, 2019 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for December 2018: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for December 2018 were approximately NT$89.83 billion, a decrease of 8.7 percent from November 2018 and a decrease of 0.1 percent from December 2017. Revenues for January through December 2018 totaled
NT$1,031.47 billion, an increase of 5.5 percent compared to the same period in 2017.
