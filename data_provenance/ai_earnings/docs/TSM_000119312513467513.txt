 TSMC November 2013 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Dec. 10, 2013 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for November 2013: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for November 2013 were approximately NT$44.33 billion, a decrease of 14.4 percent from October 2013 and an increase of 0.1 percent over November 2012. Revenues for January through November 2013 totaled NT$547.34 billion, an increase
of 16.6 percent compared to the same period in 2012.
