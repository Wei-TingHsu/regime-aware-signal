 TSMC February 2019 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – March 8, 2019 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for February 2019: On a
consolidated basis, revenues for February 2019 were approximately NT$60.89 billion, a decrease of 22.0 percent from January 2019 and a decrease of 5.8 percent from February 2018. Revenues for January through February 2019 totaled
NT$138.98 billion, a decrease of 3.7 percent compared to the same period in 2018.
