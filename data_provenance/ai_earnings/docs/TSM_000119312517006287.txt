 TSMC December 2016 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Jan. 10, 2017 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for December 2016: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for December 2016 were approximately NT$78.11 billion, a decrease of 16.0 percent from November 2016 and an increase of 33.9 percent from December 2015. Revenues for January through December 2016 totaled
NT$947.94 billion, an increase of 12.4 percent compared to the same period in 2015.
