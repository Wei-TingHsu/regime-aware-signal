 TSMC April 2015 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – May 8, 2015 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for April 2015: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for April 2015 were approximately NT$75.33 billion, an increase of 4.2 percent from March 2015 and an increase of 21.7 percent over April 2014. Revenues for January through April 2015 totaled NT$297.36 billion, an increase of 41.5
percent compared to the same period in 2014.
