 TSMC June 2016 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – July 11, 2016 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for June 2016: On a consolidated basis,
revenues for June 2016 were approximately NT$81.39 billion, an increase of 10.6 percent from May 2016 and an increase of 35.8 percent from June 2015. Revenues for January through June 2016 totaled NT$425.31 billion, a decrease of 0.5 percent
compared to the same period in 2015.
