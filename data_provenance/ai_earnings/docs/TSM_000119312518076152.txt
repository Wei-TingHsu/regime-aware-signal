 TSMC February 2018 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – March 9, 2018 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for February 2018: On a
consolidated basis, revenues for February 2018 were approximately NT$64.64 billion, a decrease of 18.9 percent from January 2018 and a decrease of 9.5 percent from February 2017. Revenues for January through February 2018 totaled
NT$144.38 billion, a decrease of 2.5 percent compared to the same period in 2017.
