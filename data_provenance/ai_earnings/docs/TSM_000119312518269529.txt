 TSMC August 2018 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Sep. 10, 2018 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for August 2018: On a consolidated basis,
revenues for August 2018 were approximately NT$91.06 billion, an increase of 22.4 percent from July 2018 and a decrease of 0.9 percent from August 2017. Revenues for January through August 2018 totaled NT$646.78 billion, an
increase of 5.8 percent compared to the same period in 2017.
