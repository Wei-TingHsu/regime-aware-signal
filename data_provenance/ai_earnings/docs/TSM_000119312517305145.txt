 TSMC September 2017 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Oct. 6, 2017 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for September 2017: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for September 2017 were approximately NT$88.58 billion, a decrease of 3.6 percent from August 2017 and a decrease of 1.3 percent from September 2016. Revenues for January through September 2017 totaled
NT$699.88 billion, an increase of 2.1 percent compared to the same period in 2016.
