 TSMC May 2019 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – June 10, 2019 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for May 2019: On a
consolidated basis, revenues for May 2019 were approximately NT$80.44 billion, an increase of 7.7 percent from April 2019 and a decrease of 0.7 percent from May 2018. Revenues for January through May 2019 totaled
NT$373.84 billion, a decrease of 9.0 percent compared to the same period in 2018.
