 TSMC August 2017 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – September 8, 2017 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for August 2017: On
a consolidated basis, revenues for August 2017 were approximately NT$91.92 billion, an increase of 28.4 percent from July 2017 and a decrease of 2.5 percent from August 2016. Revenues for January through August 2017 totaled
NT$611.30 billion, an increase of 2.6 percent compared to the same period in 2016.
