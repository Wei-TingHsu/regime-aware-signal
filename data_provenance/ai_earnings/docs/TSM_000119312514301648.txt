 TSMC July 2014 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Aug 8, 2014 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for July 2014: On a consolidated basis,
revenues for July 2014 were approximately NT$64.93 billion, a increase of 7.6 percent from June 2014 and an increase of 24.6 percent over July 2013. Revenues for January through July 2014 totaled NT$396.16 billion, an increase of 16.3 percent
compared to the same period in 2013.
