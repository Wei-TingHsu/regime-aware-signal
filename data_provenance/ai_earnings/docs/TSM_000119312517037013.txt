 TSMC January 2017 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Feb. 10, 2017 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for January 2017: On a consolidated basis,
revenues for January 2017 were approximately NT$76.62 billion, a decrease of 1.9 percent from December 2016 and an increase of 8.1 percent from January 2016.
