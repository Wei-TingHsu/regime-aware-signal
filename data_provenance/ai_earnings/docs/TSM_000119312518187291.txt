 TSMC May 2018 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – June 8, 2018 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for May 2018: On a
consolidated basis, revenues for May 2018 were approximately NT$80.97 billion, a decrease of 1.1 percent from April 2018 and an increase of 11.2 percent from May 2017. Revenues for January through May 2018 totaled
NT$410.92 billion, an increase of 13.0 percent compared to the same period in 2017.
