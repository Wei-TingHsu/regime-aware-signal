 TSMC May 2015 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – June 10, 2015 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for May 2015: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for May 2015 were approximately NT$70.16 billion, a decrease of 6.9 percent from April 2015 and an increase of 15.4 percent over May 2014. Revenues for January through May 2015 totaled NT$367.52 billion, an increase of 35.7 percent
compared to the same period in 2014.
