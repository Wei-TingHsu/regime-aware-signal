 TSMC May 2017 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – June 9, 2017 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for May 2017: On a consolidated basis,
revenues for May 2017 were approximately NT$72.80 billion, an increase of 28.0 percent from April 2017 and a decrease of 1.1 percent from May 2016. Revenues for January through May 2017 totaled NT$363.58 billion, an increase of 5.7 percent compared
to the same period in 2016.
