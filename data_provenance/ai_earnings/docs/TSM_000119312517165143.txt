 TSMC April 2017 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – May 10, 2017 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for April 2017: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for April 2017 were approximately NT$56.87 billion, a decrease of 33.8 percent from March 2017 and a decrease of 14.9 percent from April 2016. Revenues for January through April 2017 totaled NT$290.79 billion, an increase of 7.6
percent compared to the same period in 2016.
