 TSMC April 2019 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – May 10, 2019 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for April 2019: On a
consolidated basis, revenues for April 2019 were approximately NT$74.69 billion, a decrease of 6.3 percent from March 2019 and a decrease of 8.8 percent from April 2018. Revenues for January through April 2019 totaled
NT$293.40 billion, a decrease of 11.1 percent compared to the same period in 2018.
