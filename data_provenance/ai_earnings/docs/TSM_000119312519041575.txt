 TSMC January 2019 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Feb. 15, 2019 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for January 2019: On a consolidated basis,
revenues for January 2019 were approximately NT$78.09 billion, a decrease of 13.1 percent from December 2018 and a decrease of 2.1 percent from January 2018.
