 TSMC June 2019 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – July 10, 2019 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for June 2019: On a
consolidated basis, revenues for June 2019 were approximately NT$85.87 billion, an increase of 6.8 percent from May 2019 and an increase of 21.9 percent from June 2018. Revenues for January through June 2019 totaled
NT$459.70 billion, a decrease of 4.5 percent compared to the same period in 2018.
