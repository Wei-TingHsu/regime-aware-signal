 TSMC March 2018 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – April 10, 2018 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for March 2018: On a
consolidated basis, revenues for March 2018 were approximately NT$103.70 billion, an increase of 60.4 percent from February 2018 and an increase of 20.8 percent from March 2017. Revenues for January through March 2018 totaled
NT$248.08 billion, an increase of 6.1 percent compared to the same period in 2017.
