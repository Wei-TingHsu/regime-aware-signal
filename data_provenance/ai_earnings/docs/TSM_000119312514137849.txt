 TSMC March 2014 Revenue Report 

 Hsin c hu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – April 10 , 20 14 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net
revenues for March 2014: On a consolidated basis, revenues for March 2014 were approximately NT$49.96 billion, an increase of 6.7 percent from February 2014 and an increase of 13.2 percent over March 2013. Revenues for January through March 2014
totaled NT$148.22 billion, an increase of 11.7 percent compared to the same period in 2013.
