 TSMC July 2016 Revenue Report 

 Hsin c hu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Aug 10, 2016 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for July
2016: On a consolidated basis, revenues for July 2016 were approximately NT$76.39 billion, a decrease of 6.1 percent from June 2016 and a decrease of 5.6 percent from July 2015. Revenues for January through July 2016 totaled NT$501.70 billion, a
decrease of 1.3 percent compared to the same period in 2015.
