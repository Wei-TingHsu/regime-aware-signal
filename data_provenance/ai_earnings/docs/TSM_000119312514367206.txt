 TSMC September 2014 Revenue Report 

 Hsin c hu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Oct 9 , 20 14 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net
revenues for September 2014: On a consolidated basis, revenues for September 2014 were approximately NT$74.85 billion, a increase of 8.0 percent from August 2014 and an increase of 35.1 percent over September 2013. Revenues for January through
September 2014 totaled NT$540.29 billion, an increase of 19.7 percent compared to the same period in 2013.
