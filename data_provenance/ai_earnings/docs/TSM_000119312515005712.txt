 TSMC December 2014 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Jan. 9, 2014 – TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for December 2014: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for December 2014 were approximately NT$69.51 billion, a decrease of 3.8 percent from November 2014 and an increase of 39.9 percent over December 2013. Revenues for January through December 2014 totaled NT$762.81 billion, an increase
of 27.8 percent compared to the same period in 2013.
