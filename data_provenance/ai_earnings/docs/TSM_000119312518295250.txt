 TSMC September 2018 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Oct. 9, 2018 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for September 2018: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for September 2018 were approximately NT$94.92 billion, an increase of 4.2 percent from August 2018 and an increase of 7.2 percent from September 2017. Revenues for January through September 2018 totaled
NT$741.70 billion, an increase of 6.0 percent compared to the same period in 2017.
