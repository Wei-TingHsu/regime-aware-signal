 TSMC March 2015 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – April 10, 2015 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for March 2015: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for March 2015 were approximately NT$72.27 billion, an increase of 15.4 percent from February 2015 and an increase of 44.7 percent over March 2014. Revenues for January through March 2015 totaled NT$222.03 billion, an increase of
49.8 percent compared to the same period in 2014.
