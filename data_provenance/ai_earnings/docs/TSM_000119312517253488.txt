 TSMC July 2017 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – August 10, 2017 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for July 2017: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for July 2017 were approximately NT$71.61 billion, a decrease of 14.9 percent from June 2017 and a decrease of 6.3 percent from July 2016. Revenues for January through July 2017 totaled NT$519.38 billion, an increase of 3.5 percent
compared to the same period in 2016.
