 TSMC Board of Directors Meeting Resolutions 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C., August 8, 2017 – TSMC (NYSE: TSM) today held a meeting of the Board of Directors, which approved
capital appropriations of approximately US$3,153.6 million for purposes including: 1) Construction of fab facilities for US$528 million; 2) Other purposes for US$2,625.6 million including: Expanding and upgrading advanced
technology equipment; Expanding advanced packaging technology capacity; Upgrading specialty technology capacity; Conversion of logic capacity to specialty technology; Fourth quarter 2017 R&D capital investments and sustaining capital
expenditures.
