 Clarification of media reports 

 TSMC always respects and obeys the law. We take seriously any allegation that is brought to our attention. Our procedures, employee training and control
mechanisms are established to ensure that we comply with all legal rules and regulations. The semiconductor industry is a highly dynamic and competitive industry, which means TSMC is constantly having to work hard, to make investments in R&D and
capacity, and to deliver high quality and innovative services to our customers. Therefore, we believe any allegation accusing us of engaging in any anticompetitive conduct is without merit. 

 To the extent any regulatory agency requests information of TSMC, it is TSMC’s practice to respond cooperatively. As of now, TSMC has received no
official request for information from any legal authority regarding the allegation.
