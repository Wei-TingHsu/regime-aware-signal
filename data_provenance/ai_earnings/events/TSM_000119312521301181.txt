 TSMC Announces Proposed Offering of Senior Unsecured Notes 

 On October 18, 2021, TSMC Arizona Corporation (“TSMC Arizona”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited
(TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) (“TSMC”), announced that it proposes to offer U.S. dollar-denominated senior unsecured notes (the “Notes”), unconditionally and irrevocably guaranteed by TSMC, subject to market and other conditions, in an
underwritten public offering registered with the United States Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”). The principal amount, interest rates, maturity dates and other terms of the Notes have not been finalized and will be determined
at the time of pricing of the offering. 
 TSMC and TSMC Arizona intend to use the net proceeds of the sale of the Notes for general corporate purposes.

 This document contains information about the pending offering of Notes, and there can be no assurance that the offering will be completed.
