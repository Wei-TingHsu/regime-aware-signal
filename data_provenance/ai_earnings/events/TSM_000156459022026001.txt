 TSMC Announces Pricing of US$400 Million 4.375% and US$600 Million 4.625% Senior Unsecured Notes

 On July 19, 2022 New York time, TSMC Global Ltd. (“TSMC Global”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (“TSMC”), priced its private offering of US$1 billion aggregate principal amount of senior unsecured notes. The offering consists of:

 (1)

 US$400 million aggregate principal amount of 4.375% notes due July 22, 2027 (offered at a price of 99.951% of par); and

 (2)

 US$600 million aggregate principal amount of 4.625% notes due July 22, 2032 (offered at a price of 99.124% of par).

 The offering is expected to close on July 22, 2022 subject to customary closing conditions. The notes will be unconditionally and irrevocably guaranteed by TSMC (the “Guarantees”).

 Net proceeds from this offering are approximately US$992 million. TSMC and TSMC Global intend to use the net proceeds from this offering for general corporate purposes.
