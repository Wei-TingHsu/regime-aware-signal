 TSMC October 20 15 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Nov. 10, 2015 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for October 2015: On a consolidated basis,
revenues for October 2015 were approximately NT$81.74 billion, an increase of 26.7 percent from September 2015 and a gain of 1.2 percent versus October 2014. Revenues for January through October 2015 totaled NT$721.72 billion, an increase of 16.2
percent compared to the same period in 2014.
