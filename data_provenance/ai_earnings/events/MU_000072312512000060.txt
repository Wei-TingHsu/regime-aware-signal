 Item 8.01. 

 Other Events. 

 On April 11, 2012, Micron Technology, Inc. (“Micron”) issued a press release announcing the proposed private placement of $870.0 million aggregate principal amount of convertible senior notes due 2032. A copy of this press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to his report and is incorporated by reference. 

 On April 12, 2012, Micron issued a press release with respect to the pricing of its offer and sale of $870.0 million aggregate principal amount of Convertible Senior Notes due 2032. A copy of this press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.2 to this report and is incorporated herein by reference.
