 Expected Size and Implementation Plan of Shareholder Returns for Fiscal Year 2026 of

 SK hynix Inc. (the “Company”) (Fair Disclosure) 

 1. Information 

 Title 

 Expected Size and Implementation Plan of Shareholder Returns for Fiscal Year 2026 

 Details 

 - Expected Size of
Shareholder Returns for Fiscal Year 2026: Approximately Won 100 trillion

 •

 Cash dividends of Won 30 trillion or more, and acquisition and cancellation of treasury shares of Won 60 trillion or more.

 •

 Inclusive of the Won 40 trillion acquisition and cancellation of treasury shares resolved by the Company’s board of directors (the “Board
of Directors”) in August 2026; and exclusive of treasury shares acquired for compensation for executive officers and employees purposes.

 •

 To be implemented sequentially through the payment date of annual dividends for fiscal year 2026.

 - Expansion of Cash Dividends

 •

 Dividends for the third quarter of 2026: Won 20,000 per share (consisting of Won 10,000 reflecting earnings and Won 10,000 as a special dividend from
gains relating to its indirect investment in Kioxia Holdings Corporation (“Kioxia”)).

 •

 The Company plans to return most of its Kioxia investment gains through special dividends to be paid in two installments, as part of the dividends
for the third quarter of 2026 and the annual dividends for fiscal year 2026 (annual dividends will be determined through applicable procedures following the finalization of fiscal year-end financial
results).

 - Continued Acquisition and Cancellation of Treasury
Shares

 •

 The Company’s Board of Directors resolved to acquire Won 20 trillion of treasury shares on October 28, 2026.

 •

 Approximately Won 10 trillion is expected to be used for shareholder return purposes and approximately Won 10 trillion for compensation for executive
officers and employees purposes.

 •

 Going forward, the scale of treasury share acquisitions will be determined through quarterly resolutions of the Board of Directors, taking into
account market conditions, the Company’s cash flow and other factors, and the treasury shares acquired for shareholder return purposes are expected to be cancelled.

 * The total
shareholder return amount for 2026 and other matters constitute forward-looking information, including projections, forecasts, plans and expectations, and actual results may differ depending on future business conditions and performance.

 * The amount allocated for equity-based compensation for executive officers and
employees purposes remains subject to change depending on the Company’s future determination of the size of such compensation.
