 TSMC Board of Directors Meeting Resolutions 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – May 14, 2013 – TSMC (NYSE: TSM) today held a meeting of the Board of Directors, which passed the following
resolutions: 

 1. 
 Approved capital appropriations totaling approximately US4,901.9 million for the purpose of R&D and capacity expansion for advanced process
technologies, construction of 12-inch GigaFab TM
 facilities, and installation of facility systems. 

 2. 
 Approved the issuance of unsecured straight corporate bonds in the domestic market for an amount not exceeding NT$45 billion to raise capital for capacity expansion.
