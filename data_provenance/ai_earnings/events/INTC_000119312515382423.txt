 Item 7.01 
 Regulation FD Disclosure 
 On November 19, 2015, Intel Corporation (“Intel”) presented
business and financial information to institutional investors, analysts, members of the press and the general public at a publicly available webcast meeting (the “Investor Meeting”). Attached hereto as exhibits and incorporated by
reference herein are the Investor Meeting presentations made by Brian Krzanich, Chief Executive Officer; William Holt, Executive Vice President, General Manager of the Technology and Manufacturing Group; Stacy Smith, Executive Vice President and
Chief Financial Officer; Diane Bryant, Senior Vice President, General Manager of the Data Center Group; and Kirk Skaugen, Senior Vice President, General Manager of the Client Computing Group, respectively. During the course of the Investor Meeting,
Intel’s executives discussed the company’s corporate strategy, advancing Moore’s Law, financial performance, and business updates. The presentations include forward-looking statements and accompanying Risk Factors. These presentations
are among the several presentations made by Intel executives at the Investor Meeting, each of which may be found at intc.com. 
 The information in this
report shall not be treated as filed for purposes of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.
