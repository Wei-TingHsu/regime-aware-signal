 Item 8.01. 
 Other Events. 

 On March 20, 2019, the European Commission (EC) announced its decision that certain contractual provisions in agreements that Google LLC, a wholly-owned
subsidiary of Alphabet Inc. (“Alphabet”), had with AdSense for Search (AFS) partners infringed European competition law. The EC decision imposes a €1.49 billion fine and directs actions relating to the AFS agreements. Alphabet expects
that it will accrue the fine in the first quarter of 2019.
