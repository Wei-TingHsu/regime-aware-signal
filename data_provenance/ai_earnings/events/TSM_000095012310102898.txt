 TSMC Board of Directors Resolutions 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. — November 9, 2010 — TSMC (NYSE: TSM) today held a meeting of the Board of
Directors, which passed the following resolutions:

 1. 

 Approved capital appropriations of US$1,880.9 million to set up mini-lines for the wafer fab
in Taichung, and to expand advanced technology capacity, twelve-inch capacity for wafer-level
chip-scale packaging, and specialty technology capacity. 

 2. 

 Approved 2011 R&D and sustaining capital appropriation of US$803.76 million. 

 3. 

 Approved capital injection of 9.4 million euros into TSMC Solar Europe B.V.
