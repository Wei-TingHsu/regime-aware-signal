 TSMC Shareholders Approve NT$3.0 Cash Dividend 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. — June 15, 2010 — TSMC (NYSE: TSM) today held a shareholders’ meeting at
which passed the following major resolutions:

 1. 

 Approved the 2009 Business Report and Financial Statements. Consolidated revenue for 2009
totaled NT$295.74 billion and net income was NT$89.22 billion, with earnings per share of
NT$3.44. 

 2. 

 Approved the distribution of a NT$3.0 cash dividend per common share. 

 3. 

 Approved amendments to TSMC’s Articles of Incorporation expanding the company’s business
scope to encompass solid-state lighting and solar power.
