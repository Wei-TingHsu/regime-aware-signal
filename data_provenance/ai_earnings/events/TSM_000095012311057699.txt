 TSMC Sets July 5 Record Date for Common Share Dividends 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. — June 9, 2011 — TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today set July 5, 2011 as
the record date for its common stock shareholders entitled to participate in distribution of 2010
profits in the form of cash dividend. The ex-dividend date for TSMC common shares shall be June 29,
2011. As required by Article 165 of Taiwan’s Company Law, the shareholders’ register shall be
closed for five days prior to the record date (July 1 through July 5, 2011) for registration
transfer.

 The ex-dividend date for TSMC American Depositary Shares (ADSs) will also be June 29, 2011. The
record date for TSMC ADSs entitled to participate in this cash dividend distribution will be July
1, 2011. Holders of ADSs are encouraged to check with their securities brokers for receipt of the
cash dividend in their own accounts. Any questions regarding this upcoming dividend may be directed
to Ms. Emi Mak at CitiBank, N.A. (Tel: +1-212-816-6693; email: emi.p.mak@citi.com)
