 TSMC Sets July 2 as Record Date for Common Share Dividends 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – June 8, 2017 – TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today set July 2, 2017 as the record date for its
common stock shareholders entitled to participate in distribution of 2016 profits in the form of cash dividend. The ex-dividend date for TSMC common shares shall be June 26, 2017. As required by Article 165 of
Taiwan’s Company Law, the shareholders’ register shall be closed for five days prior to the record date (June 28 through July 2, 2017) for registration transfer. 

 The ex-dividend date for TSMC American Depositary Shares (ADSs) will be June 26. The record date for TSMC ADSs
entitled to participate in this cash dividend distribution will be June 28. Holders of ADSs are encouraged to check with their securities brokers for receipt of the cash dividend in their own accounts. Any questions regarding this upcoming dividend
may be directed to Mr. Ganesh Sarpotdar at Citibank, N.A. (Tel: +1-212-816-6783; email: ganesh.sarpotdar@citi.com)
