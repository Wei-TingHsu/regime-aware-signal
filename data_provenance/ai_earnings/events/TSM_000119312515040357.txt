 TSMC January 20 15 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Feb. 10, 2015 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for January 2015: On a consolidated basis,
revenues for January 2015 were approximately NT$87.12 billion, an increase of 25.3 percent from December 2014 and an increase of 69.4 percent over January 2014.
