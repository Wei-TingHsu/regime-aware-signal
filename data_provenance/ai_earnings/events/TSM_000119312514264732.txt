 TSMC June 20 14 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – July 10, 2014 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for June 2014: On a consolidated
basis, revenues for June 2014 were approximately NT$60.34 billion, a decrease of 0.7 percent from May 2014 and an increase of 11.7 percent over June 2013. Revenues for January through June 2014 totaled NT$331.24 billion, an increase of 14.8 percent
compared to the same period in 2013.
