 TSMC Shareholders Approve NT$3.0 Cash Dividend 

 and Transfer of Solar and Solid State Lighting Businesses 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. — June 9, 2011 — TSMC (NYSE: TSM) today held a shareholders’ meeting,
which passed the following major resolutions:

 1. 

 Approved the 2010 Business Report and Financial Statements. Consolidated revenue for 2010
totaled NT$419.54 billion and net income was NT$161.61 billion, with earnings per share of
NT$6.23. 

 2. 

 Approved the distribution of a NT$3.0 cash dividend per common share. 

 3. 

 Approved the transfer of TSMC’s solar business and solid state lighting business into two new
TSMC wholly-owned companies respectively. 

 4. 

 Elected Ms. Kok-Choo Chen and Mr. Gregory C. Chow to the Board of Directors as additional
Independent Directors.
