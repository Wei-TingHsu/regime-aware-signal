 Item 7.01 
 Regulation FD Disclosure. 
 On
September 12, 2012, Intel Corporation (“Intel”) closed its purchase of 62,977,877 ordinary shares of ASML Holding N.V. (“ASML”) for €2.5 billion (approximately $3.1 billion). The transaction is part of the previously
disclosed series of agreements between Intel and ASML under which Intel is helping to fund ASML’s research and development programs to accelerate deployment of new technologies for 450-millimeter (mm) wafers and extreme ultra-violet (EUV)
lithography. The information in this report shall not be treated as filed for purposes of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.
