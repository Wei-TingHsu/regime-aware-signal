 TSMC Shareholders Approve NT$6 Cash Dividend 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – June 7, 2016 – TSMC (NYSE: TSM) today held its 2016 Annual Shareholders’ Meeting, which passed the
following major resolutions: 

 1. 
 Approved amendments to TSMC’s Articles of Incorporation. 

 2. 
 Acknowledged the 2015 Business Report and Financial Statements. Consolidated revenue for 2015 totaled NT$843.5 billion and net income was NT$306.57 billion with earnings per share of NT$11.82. 

 3. 
 Approved the distribution of a NT$6 cash dividend per common share.
