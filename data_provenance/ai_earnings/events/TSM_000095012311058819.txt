 Announcement and notice to creditors of solar and solid state lighting business transfer

 On June 9, 2011, the shareholders of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company, Ltd (“TSMC”)
approved the transfer of TSMC’s solar and solid state lighting businesses into two new wholly owned
subsidiaries: TSMC Solar Ltd. and TSMC Solid State Lighting Ltd., respectively (the “Transfer”).
The Record Date of the Transfer is August 1, 2011. The Transfer will have no effect on business
transactions between TSMC and its creditors.

 This is to notify TSMC’s creditors of the Transfer pursuant to Article 32 of the Business Mergers
and Acquisitions Law of the R.O.C.. Creditors shall inform TSMC in writing of any objections they
may have to the Transfer by mailing their objections, together with the related credit
certificates, to the following address no later than July 18, 2011 (as certified by the postage
stamp):

 TSMC Financial Planning Dept. of Finance Division

8, Li-Hsin Rd. 6, Hsinchu Science Park,

Hsinchu, Taiwan 300-77, R.O.C.

 Any objections raised after July 18, 2011 will be deemed ineffective.
