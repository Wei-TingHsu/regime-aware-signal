 TSMC Board of Directors Meeting Resolutions 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C., November 14, 2017 – TSMC (NYSE: TSM) today held a meeting of the Board of Directors, which passed the
following resolutions: 

 1. 
 Approved capital appropriations of approximately US$4,284.5 million for purposes including: 1) US$1,667.5 million for construction of fab facilities; 2) US$2,617 million for other purposes
including: Expanding and upgrading advanced technology capacity; Expanding advanced packaging technology capacity; Expanding specialty technology capacity; Conversion of logic capacity to specialty technology; First quarter 2018 R&D capital
investments and sustaining capital expenditures. 

 2. 
 Approved the capital injection of not more than US$2 billion to TSMC Global Ltd., a wholly-owned BVI subsidiary, for the purpose of reducing foreign exchange hedging costs.
