 TSMC Shareholders Approve NT$3.0 Cash Dividend 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – June 24, 2014 – TSMC (NYSE: TSM) today held its annual shareholders’ meeting, which passed the following
major resolutions: 

 1. 
 Approved the 2013 Business Report and Financial Statements. Consolidated revenue for 2013 totaled approximately NT$597.02 billion and net income was approximately NT$188.15 billion, with earnings per share of NT$7.26.

 2. 
 Approved the distribution of a NT$3.0 cash dividend per common share.
