 TSMC August 20 13 Revenue Report 

 Hsinchu, Taiwan, R.O.C. – Sept. 10, 2013 - TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenues for August 2013: On a consolidated basis,
revenues for August 2013 were approximately NT$55.09 billion, an increase of 5.7 percent from July 2013 and an increase of 11.2 percent over August 2012. Revenues for January through August 2013 totaled NT$395.84 billion, an increase of 19.3 percent
compared to the same period in 2012.
