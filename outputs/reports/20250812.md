## 4. What this system cannot see

**The political source is biased toward DECIDED policy.** The Federal Register carries executive orders and proclamations — all high-specificity by construction. Statements, posts and rhetoric, precisely where a market-moving threat-to-act lives, are **not covered**. **An absence of low-specificity political events in any result below is a COVERAGE GAP, not evidence that rhetoric does not move markets.**
